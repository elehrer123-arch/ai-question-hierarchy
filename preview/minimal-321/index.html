3.2.1 · Task and occupation change

Which tasks and occupations will be automated, augmented, transformed, or newly created — and how quickly?

Exposure studies flag most occupations as partially exposed; real usage data shows adoption concentrated in software, writing, and analysis. The augmentation-vs-automation split within tasks is the number to watch.

What changed recently · reviewed August 2, 2026
  • Direct project-automation performance roughly doubled in one model generation (Remote Labor Index) — a capability fact, not yet a labor-market one.
  • Occupational and usage evidence keeps showing restructured work and shifted hiring rather than employment decline (Chicago Fed; Anthropic's June index; Narayanan & Kapoor).
  • One lab now hosts both readings: Amodei proposes displacement-pegged income support while McCrory, on internal data, reports no visible displacement in 18 months.
