5.2.4

Cognitive inequality

Will personalized tutors equalize opportunity, or give already advantaged people far greater leverage?

Bloom's two-sigma promise is finally testable at scale, and early RCTs show large gains — for the students who engage. Whether tutoring compresses or stretches the distribution depends on who engages.

What changed · December 2025–August 2026 · latest sweep August 3, 2026 · editorial review pending
01

The equalize-or-widen question got strong data pulling both ways. Purpose-built tutors matched or beat human tutors in a UK classroom RCT and produced large gains for disadvantaged students in Nigeria — the case that scalable tutoring could compress the distribution. But real-world use runs the other way in places: lower-income applicants lean more on AI for admissions essays and end up more homogenized, a case where unequal use widens rather than narrows gaps.
