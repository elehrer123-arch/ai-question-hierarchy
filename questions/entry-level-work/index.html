Entry-level work

What happens if junior coding, analysis, research, design, legal, and administrative work disappears before senior roles?

One prominent study finds relative employment declines for young workers in the most AI-exposed occupations since late 2022, concentrated where AI automates rather than augments; critics attribute the pattern to interest-rate and remote-work confounds.
