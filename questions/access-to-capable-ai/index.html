4.5.6

Access

Who should receive access to the most capable systems — and on what terms?

Compute-based governance assumes a Compute North; most of the world is Compute South. Access tiers, pricing, and eligibility decisions at a handful of firms currently function as de facto global policy.

What changed · October 2025–August 2026 · latest sweep August 3, 2026 · editorial review pending
01

The access gap got measured: roughly one in six people worldwide now use generative AI, but Global North adoption grew nearly twice as fast in late 2025, widening the divide — and the US hosts over ten times more data centers than any other country. Single company pricing decisions, like a free tier for all of India, still function as de facto global access policy.
