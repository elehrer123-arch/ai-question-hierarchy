Critical-system autonomy

Under what conditions should AI systems be permitted to operate factories, financial infrastructure, electrical grids, communications networks, and other safety-critical systems?

Automated trading gave the preview: autonomy in critical systems means failures propagate at machine speed. The design questions are which decisions must stay mechanically reversible, which require human confirmation, and who certifies either before deployment. (Weapons and military systems are treated under Power 4.3.)

What changed
December 2025–August 2026 · swept August 3, 2026 · editorial review pending
01

The question is being answered first in guidance rather than deployment. US and allied agencies issued joint advice on integrating AI into operational technology, organized around risk assessment, model governance, and fail-safes that let systems 'fail gracefully'; the academic proposals converge on 'bounded autonomy' — matching the degree of human oversight to a sector's complexity and potential harm.
