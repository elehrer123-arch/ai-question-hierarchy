4.2.2

Government use

How should AI be deployed in welfare, taxation, policing, courts, immigration, intelligence, and public administration?

Agency adoption reached far further by 2020 than most realized — mostly unglamorous, mostly ungoverned. The live split is between procedural-safeguard approaches (due process, contestability) and use-case prohibitions, while adoption outpaces both.

What changed · January–August 2026 · latest sweep August 3, 2026 · editorial review pending
01

Adoption ran ahead of governance: 3.4 million federal users can now reach frontier chatbots through GSA's OneGov deals, and the first major ruling held that an agency's chatbot-driven classifications are the government's own act for constitutional purposes — outsourcing a decision to a model does not launder state action. The deeper problem now has a research agenda: automating the executive branch removes the human friction that checks presidential power.
