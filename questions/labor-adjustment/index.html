Adjustment

Can education, retraining, labor mobility, credentialing, and social insurance adapt quickly enough?

Labor-market institutions adapt on decade timescales; AI capability moves faster. The policy inventory — retraining, wage insurance, credential reform — exists on paper and is largely untested at speed.
