Military integration

How should AI be used in intelligence, cyber operations, autonomous weapons, targeting, command systems, and nuclear decision-making?

Wargaming studies find LLMs tend to escalate, occasionally to nuclear use, and the ICRC argues for legal limits on weapon autonomy before integration becomes routine. Command-and-intelligence integration is proceeding faster than doctrine either way; the nuclear question is the sharpest.
