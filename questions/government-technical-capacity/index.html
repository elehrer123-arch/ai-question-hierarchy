Technical competence

Can governments recruit enough talent to evaluate systems, investigate incidents, procure AI, and challenge industry claims?

Government pay scales and clearance timelines lose to lab compensation by an order of magnitude. AI safety institutes are the current workaround: small, technical, and deliberately outside normal civil-service structures.
