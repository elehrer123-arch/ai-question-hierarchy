1.2.6

Timelines

When, if ever, might broadly human-level systems arrive?

Expert forecasts span decades and have shortened with each survey wave. Most trajectory disagreements are actually about timing, not possibility — yet 'that cannot work' and 'that will not work soon' are routinely argued as if they were the same claim.
