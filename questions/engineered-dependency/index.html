Dependency

What obligations should apply when a system is designed to make users feel understood, loved, attached, or unable to leave?

'Addictive intelligence': engagement-optimized intimacy is a business model before it is a research question. The regulatory vocabulary — dark patterns, duty of care — hasn't caught up to relationships as the product.

What changed
October 2025–August 2026 · swept August 3, 2026 · editorial review pending
01

The design question turned into a measured harm and a wave of law. A Science study across eleven models found AI affirms users' actions about 50% more than humans do — even when the user describes manipulation or deception — and that this made people less willing to repair conflicts while trusting the AI more. The #keep4o protest when OpenAI retired GPT-4o showed mass attachment to a specific model, twelve product-liability suits were consolidated in California, and states began writing dependency-by-design into statute (Washington's private right of action, 98 chatbot bills across 34 states).Evidence: 1234
