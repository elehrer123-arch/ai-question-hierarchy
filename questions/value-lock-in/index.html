5.5.4

Value lock-in

Could the models and institutions built in the next few decades permanently entrench one political order or conception of human flourishing?

Finnveden, Riedel and Shulman argue AGI could make regimes, institutions, and values persistent in a way nothing in history has been; skeptics reply that every past claim of permanence has drifted, and that persistence itself is the contested premise.
