5.2.1

Curriculum

What should people learn when explanation, translation, coding, calculation, and factual retrieval are nearly free?

When execution is nearly free, judgment, taste, and problem-formulation rise in relative value — but almost no curriculum has been redesigned around that inversion yet.

What changed · October 2025–August 2026 · latest sweep August 3, 2026 · editorial review pending
01

The curriculum conversation converged on an inversion: as AI removes the friction between question and answer, judgment and taste rise in relative value and become the thing worth teaching. The constructive move is to define the new literacy concretely — Anthropic's fluency index operationalizes skillful AI use as observable collaboration behaviors like iteration and refinement, rather than one-shot answer extraction.
