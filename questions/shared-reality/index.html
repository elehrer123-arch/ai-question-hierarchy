5.1.2

Shared reality

Can journalism, science, courts, universities, and government preserve trusted procedures for establishing facts?

Epistemic security reframes truth decay as an infrastructure problem — the institutions that certify facts need defending the way power grids do, not the way individual claims do.

01

The most encouraging news for shared epistemic procedures came from crowd fact-checking: a real deployment study and an independent seven-month evaluation both found AI-written Community Notes earning cross-partisan 'helpful' ratings at roughly double the human rate — evidence AI can strengthen, not only erode, collective fact-finding. Institutions are also building working defenses, from Wikipedia's AI-cleanup guides to updated epistemic-security frameworks.
