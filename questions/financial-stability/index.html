Financial stability

Could synchronized agents, correlated models, concentrated investment, automated trading, or an AI-capex reversal create systemic risks?

Regulators flag herding from correlated models, dependence on a few providers, and the sheer scale of AI capital expenditure as systemic scenarios — a financial-stability watchlist that barely existed two years ago.
