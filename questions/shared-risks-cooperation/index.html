What changed
November 2025–August 2026 · swept August 3, 2026 · editorial review pending
01

The two-year freeze ended: the US and China announced an intergovernmental dialogue on AI safety, and April's IDAIS-London statement shows where rival states' scientists still co-sign — near-term AI cyber capability handing non-state actors state-level attack capacity, with biological misuse close behind. The design question is now which cooperation format survives rivalry: standing information-sharing exchanges rather than arms-control bargaining, even as the one existing military commitment (human control of nuclear decisions) loses definition.
