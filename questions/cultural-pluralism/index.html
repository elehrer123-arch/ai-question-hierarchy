5.4.5

Pluralism

Will AI enable more niche cultures and voices, or homogenize expression around the tastes and values of a few dominant systems?

The Doshi-Hauser result: generative AI raises individual creativity while reducing collective diversity. Algorithmic monoculture generalizes the worry — many actors, one model, converging outputs.
