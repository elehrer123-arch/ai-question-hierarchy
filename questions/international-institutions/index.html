4.4.3

Institutions

Should cooperation rely on existing organizations or require a new body analogous to the IAEA?

IAEA, CERN, and IPCC analogies each fit a different function — inspection, joint research, consensus assessment. Regime-complex thinking predicts many overlapping bodies rather than one AI agency.

What changed · October 2025–August 2026 · latest sweep August 3, 2026 · editorial review pending
01

The institutional landscape actually changed: 29 countries signed the treaty establishing the World AI Cooperation Organization in Shanghai — expert readings treat it as China's counterweight to Western-led bodies — while the UN's IPCC-analog scientific panel delivered its first report, warning that by the time evidence is clear it may be too late to act on it. The summit series, meanwhile, reoriented from safety toward diffusion and development at New Delhi.
