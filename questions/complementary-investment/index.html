Complementary investment

How much value requires new infrastructure, data systems, robotics, training, and organizational change?

The general-purpose-technology literature argues intangible investment — process redesign, data, retraining — historically dwarfs spending on the technology itself; whether AI follows that pattern or diffuses more cheaply is the open question.
